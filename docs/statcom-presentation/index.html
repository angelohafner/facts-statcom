05 / Reactive power

Capacitive mode: inject VARs

At zero relative angle, increasing the converter fundamental voltage makes reactive current flow toward the network.

05
Voltage and current phasorsQ > 0
TAKE A SNAPSHOT
When E > V and δ = 0:
Qinj > 0

The injected current phasor points at −90° relative to the PCC voltage for our converter-to-grid current direction.

Remember the direction

If you define current into the STATCOM instead, its phasor sign reverses. Reactive-power convention does not change.

06 / Reactive power

Inductive mode: absorb VARs

Reduce the converter fundamental voltage below the PCC voltage and reactive current reverses.

06
Voltage and current phasorsQ < 0
TAKE A SNAPSHOT
When E < V and δ = 0:
Qinj < 0

The injected current phasor now points at +90° relative to the PCC voltage.

Physical meaning

At the fundamental frequency, the STATCOM behaves like a controllable reactive current sink.
